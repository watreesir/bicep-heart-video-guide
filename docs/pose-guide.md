# Bicep Heart Pose Guide

The bicep heart is a **one-person pose**. You flex one arm and press your other hand against the inside of the bicep, so the arm and the hand form one heart. The original trend clips are solo selfies. Couple and pet versions are creative spin-offs, not the original format.

## The original: solo bicep heart

Based on [BoxLife](https://boxlifemagazine.com/bicep-heart-trend-tiktok-viral/), [Mathrubhumi](https://english.mathrubhumi.com/lifestyle/bicep-heart-trend-how-to-try-viral-challenge-j0qbfi6w), and [ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend):

1. Face the camera. Many clips open with a glance or a shy smile before the move ([MimicZip](https://mimiczip.com/bicep-heart), [ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)).
2. Make a fist and bend that arm up so the fist is raised near your head.
3. Bring your other hand across your chest and place it against the inside of the flexed bicep.
4. Point your thumb down, pressing into the muscle, and curl your fingers over the arm. Your hand makes one half of the heart; the curve of the flexed arm makes the other.
5. Turn the arm slightly toward the camera until the gap between arm and hand reads as a heart, and hold it for a beat.

Most videos use the same TikTok sound ("original sound - trendformusic"), and many open with the line "Have you ever heard of a bicep heart…" ([BoxLife](https://boxlifemagazine.com/bicep-heart-trend-tiktok-viral/), [ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)).

You don't need big arms. The hand placement does most of the shape-forming work ([BoxLife](https://boxlifemagazine.com/bicep-heart-trend-tiktok-viral/)), and small-arm versions are part of the joke ([ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)).

## Spin-offs (creative extensions, not the original)

### Couple version
In popular couple clips, one partner, often the boyfriend, sends the solo bicep heart and the other partner's reaction is filmed. The heart is still made by one person's arm and hand ([ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)). If two people each make their own bicep heart side by side, that's a further creative variation.

### Pet version
Pets don't have biceps. AI tools that offer a pet mode produce an approximation, such as the pet standing upright with a paw curled toward a heart ([ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)). Some tools don't offer pets at all, because the heart needs an arm and a hand ([MimicZip](https://mimiczip.com/bicep-heart)). Treat this as a fun AI variation, not something a real pet can perform.

## Filming tips
- Vertical 9:16 selfie framing, chest or waist up, with the arm and hand fully in frame ([MimicZip](https://mimiczip.com/bicep-heart), [ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)).
- If the frame is cut off at the chin, the heart is lost ([ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)).

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix (from the descriptions above) |
|---|---|
| Hand on the outside of the arm | Place it against the **inside** of the flexed bicep |
| Thumb pointing up | Thumb points **down**, fingers curled over |
| Heart doesn't read on camera | Angle the arm toward the lens |
| Arm cropped out | Frame from the chest or waist up |

## Bicep heart vs finger heart
A finger heart is a small heart made by crossing your thumb and index finger. The bicep heart uses a flexed arm plus the other hand. See [faq.md](faq.md).
