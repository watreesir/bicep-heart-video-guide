# Bicep Heart FAQ

**What is the bicep heart?**
It's a one-person pose: you flex one arm with the fist near your head and press your other hand, thumb down and fingers curled, against the inside of that bicep, so arm and hand form a heart. The #bicepheart hashtag appeared on TikTok on October 26, 2026 ([BoxLife](https://boxlifemagazine.com/bicep-heart-trend-tiktok-viral/)).

**How do I do the bicep heart pose?**
Flex one arm, bring the other hand across your chest onto the inside of the bicep, point your thumb down, curl your fingers, and angle the arm toward the camera ([Mathrubhumi](https://english.mathrubhumi.com/lifestyle/bicep-heart-trend-how-to-try-viral-challenge-j0qbfi6w)). See [pose-guide.md](pose-guide.md).

**Do I need big biceps?**
No. The hand does most of the shape-forming work ([BoxLife](https://boxlifemagazine.com/bicep-heart-trend-tiktok-viral/)).

**Is there a couple or pet version?**
These are spin-offs, not the original. Couple clips usually show one partner sending a bicep heart and the other reacting ([ViralRemix](https://viralremix.org/bicep-heart-trend)). Pets have no biceps, so pet versions exist only as AI approximations, and some tools don't offer them at all ([MimicZip](https://mimiczip.com/bicep-heart)).

**Bicep heart vs finger heart: what's the difference?**
A finger heart is a small heart made by crossing thumb and index finger. A bicep heart uses whole flexed arms and is usually done by two people.

**Is there an official bicep heart app?**
No. The trend has no official app. TikTok's own effects, general AI video apps (such as Kling or Dreamina/Seedance), and independent tools like [BicepHeart.tools](https://bicepheart.tools/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=bicep-heart) (made by the authors of this guide) are all options. None of them are affiliated with the trend's creators.

**Which AI model is best for bicep heart videos?**
Public tests don't show a clear winner. Each has strengths. See [model-notes.md](model-notes.md).

**Is it OK to animate someone else's photo?**
Only with their consent. Label AI clips as AI-generated.
