# Contributing

Improvements to the docs are welcome: corrections, clearer steps, translations, and new public sources.

- **Cite a source** for any factual claim, such as trend history, pose description, or model capability or price. Include the URL and your access date.
- **Keep facts, third-party reports, and inferences separate** in `docs/model-notes.md` (layers A, B, C).
- **Don't add third-party videos, screenshots, or likenesses.** That includes TikTok or X clips, creators' faces, and celebrity images. Only original diagrams or AI-generated, clearly labeled examples made with consent.
- **No promotional rankings.** Don't add "best tool" claims or affiliate links.

Open an issue or a pull request. By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
